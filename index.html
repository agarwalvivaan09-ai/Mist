Every part has a job.

MIST is 12 cm of metal that does one thing: keeps the fragrance you already own within reach.

  • Cap Snaps on over the nozzle so nothing leaks into your bag.
  • Silver ring A polished trim where the cap meets the body.
  • Metal shell Glossy black or silver. Slim enough for a jeans pocket.
  • Fill window Shows how much fragrance is left. Each fill gives 30–35 sprays.2
  • Bottom valve Press it onto your perfume's spray stem and pump. No funnel, no decanting.

A seamless capsule with rounded ends, a mirror-finish shell and a single silver ring where the cap meets the body. It feels solid in the hand and protects your fragrance in a crowded bag.

Refill in seconds.

MIST is made for people who already have a fragrance they love and want a smaller way to carry it.

  1. 1

    Remove the perfume cap

    Take off the spray head from your perfume bottle to expose the refill stem.

  2. 2

    Press MIST onto the stem

    Align the bottom valve and pump down steadily. Watch the window until the chamber fills.

  3. 3

    Carry it with confidence

    Snap the cap on and keep it in your pocket, school bag, gym kit, handbag or travel pouch.
